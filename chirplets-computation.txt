The citation you are referring to is a foundational research paper titled "Computation of some projective-chirplet-transform and metaplectic-chirplet-transform subspaces, with applications in image processing" (sometimes cited with "signal processing").
It was co-authored by Steve Mann and Shawn Becker and presented at the DSP World Symposium in Boston, Massachusetts, in November 1992.

Key Context & Significance

• The Chirplet Transform: This paper is a critical milestone in the development of the chirplet transform—a signal processing framework pioneered by Steve Mann. Unlike traditional wavelet transforms that decompose signals into fixed-frequency packets, chirplet transforms use mathematical pieces ("chirplets") that can vary in frequency over time.
• The Authors: At the time of publication, both authors were researchers at the MIT Media Laboratory. Steve Mann went on to become widely recognized as the "father of wearable computing," while Shawn Becker focused on 3D computer vision and computer graphics.
• Applications: The "projective" and "metaplectic" mathematical variations introduced in this paper allowed for advanced analysis in image processing, helping computers better track and interpret how objects deform, rotate, or change perspective relative to a camera lens.
